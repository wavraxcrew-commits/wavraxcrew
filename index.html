Services

Everything between your request and a name on the crew list.

Sourcing & Vetting

We match candidates to your vessel type, rank, and flag-state rules, then screen technical knowledge, English proficiency, sea service, and references before a single CV reaches your inbox.

Documentation & Compliance

STCW certificates, medical fitness, seaman's books, visas, and flag-state endorsements — checked and filed so nothing stalls a crew change.

Travel & Onboarding

Flights, transfers, contracts, and a pre-joining briefing, with one point of contact until your crew member signs the articles.

Why Wavrax
